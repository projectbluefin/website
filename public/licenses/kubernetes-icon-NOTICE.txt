The mark is reproduced UNMODIFIED. The published white icon is already
fill:#fff, so nothing here recolours it. On the Wolves teaser it stands in for
the single "O" of WOLVES in the main title and the "O" of "Extinction" on the
first day card, scaled and seated on the type's optical line and nothing else.
The letter it replaces remains the element's alt text, so the word is still
read as written.

Note: public/brands/kubernetes.svg is the BLUE (#326ce5) full logo and is a
different asset. The white symbolic icon is used for the Wolves title
treatment at the owner's explicit direction ("not the blue one though, just
the white symbolic one").
